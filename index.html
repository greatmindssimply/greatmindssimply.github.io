Great Minds Uploader

Great Minds Uploader is a private desktop upload utility built for the owner of the educational YouTube channels Great Minds, Simply (English) and 老馬說故事 (Traditional Chinese).

What It Does

  • Uploads locally produced educational videos and Shorts from the creator's own computer to their own YouTube channels via the official YouTube Data API v3.
  • Sets titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, subtitles, playlists, and scheduled publish times from local metadata files.
  • Runs exclusively for the channel owner's personal workflow; it is not offered to third-party users.

Google API Scopes Used

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — to upload videos and Shorts to the owner's channels.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl — to manage thumbnails, captions, playlists, and video metadata on the owner's channels.
