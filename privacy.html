Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Overview

Great Minds Uploader is a private personal-use desktop tool operated solely by the owner of the Great Minds, Simply and 老馬說故事 YouTube channels. It does not offer public accounts or collect data from external users.

Data Accessed via Google APIs

When authorized by the channel owner via Google OAuth 2.0, the tool accesses only the YouTube channel information and upload permissions needed to upload videos, thumbnails, captions, playlists, and metadata to the owner's channels.

Storage and Sharing

  • OAuth credentials and upload logs are stored strictly on the owner's local computer with restricted file permissions.
  • No Google user data is sold, rented, transferred, or shared with any third party.
  • Great Minds Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking Access & Data Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account permissions page, and local tokens can be deleted directly from the owner's computer. For deletion or privacy inquiries, contact glyphs.puppies_6v@icloud.com.
